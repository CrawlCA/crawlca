02 / DATA FLOW & PROVENANCE

A record is more

The pipeline carries identity, time and source context together. This makes an observation useful beyond the moment it was collected.

IDENTITY

Resolve the asset.

Chain and contract address establish identity. Symbols, names and logos enrich the record, while exact identity governs aggregation across sources.

TIME

Keep the clocks distinct.

A source’s creation timestamp, a collection attempt and the last successful snapshot describe different events. Preserving those distinctions keeps freshness meaningful.

LINEAGE

Keep the origin reachable.

Source-specific snapshots remain available for inspection alongside the merged view. A record can be traced back to its adapter and collection context.

03 / JEV · THE DECISION LAYER

From evidence
to a defined next step.

Jev gives the review layer an explicit decision interface: a state, a question and a bounded set of outcomes.

TypeSafe describes System One models as models built around structured, probabilistic decisions. CrawlCA applies that interface to the quality of an entire collection snapshot: what evidence deserves inspection, what can remain under observation, and when a judgment should wait.

THE DECISION INTERFACE
state + question
↓
choice + probabilities

The question defines the decision space. The evidence gives the choice its context.

COLLECTION-LEVEL REVIEW
03.1

Build a compact evidence state.

Jev receives a quality summary of the snapshot. Aggregate fields preserve uncertainty and coverage without sending a token-by-token market dossier.

01 / AVAILABILITY

Source health

Adapter status, cached state, successful and failed sources, last attempt and last success.

02 / FRESHNESS

Observation age

Snapshot time, observation time and counts of live, cached, stale and unverified records.

03 / COVERAGE

Known and unknown

Present and missing counts for market cap, valuation, liquidity, volume, holders and creation time.

04 / SCOPE

Evidence boundaries

Record counts, unassessed risk flags and provenance limitations remain part of the supplied context.

03.2

Ask one explicit review question.

“Given the available evidence, what should happen next in the review of this collection snapshot?” The outcomes are declared before inference.

PRIORITIZE HUMAN INSPECTION

Investigate the evidence.

Use this route when source errors, stale or contradictory observations, missing fields or uncertain provenance deserve closer attention. It sets a review priority for the dataset.

CONTINUE OBSERVATION

Keep the snapshot under review.

Use this route when the snapshot is useful for monitoring, its known limitations are explicit and the evidence does not indicate an immediate data-quality investigation.

WAIT FOR SUFFICIENT CONTEXT

Let better evidence arrive.

Use this route when the available snapshot is too incomplete or too old to support a meaningful review judgment. Deferral preserves the boundary between observation and inference.

03.3

Validate the decision before presentation.

A model response becomes review context only after its shape and values pass application checks. The decision remains tied to the snapshot it evaluated.

01

Contract shape

An accepted Jev model identifier, a typed choice and a supported route.

02

Probability integrity

Finite values in [0, 1], a distribution summing to one within tolerance, and a selected route with maximum probability.

03

Context binding

Model identity, snapshot time, evaluation time, record count and all-chain scope travel with the result.

READING THE OUTPUT

The displayed percentage is the probability assigned to the selected review route. It expresses a choice within this decision space. Token safety and future returns are outside that scope.

03.4

Keep inference independent of the interface.

Completed snapshots feed a bounded background worker. A new eligible snapshot and an elapsed review interval gate each attempt; one review can be in flight at a time.

BOUNDED WORK

A controlled cadence.

Duplicate snapshots and overlapping evaluations are gated. A bounded request and response budget prevents review work from growing without limit.

SEPARATE RESPONSIBILITIES

Collection keeps moving.

The model call runs without holding the source-cache lock. Browser reads consume the stored result and do not initiate another inference.

STABLE PRESENTATION

Context survives a delay.

The interface can retain an earlier validated result with its original timestamps, or keep a neutral waiting view until review context becomes available.

04 / CONTINUITY BY DESIGN

Three clocks.
One coherent workspace.

Source collection, structured review and browser presentation progress independently. The workspace connects their outputs through explicit state and timestamps.

COLLECTION
Request→Normalize→Commit

Each source carries its own last-success checkpoint. Usable observations remain inspectable between cycles.

REVIEW
Eligible state→Evaluate→Validate

New completed evidence and the review interval govern when the decision layer advances.

PRESENTATION
Read→Render→Inspect

Snapshots and source events update the interface. The 3D scene gives those observations a spatial representation.

↳

Continuity preserves context. Retained observations and review results keep their original identity and time. A stable display does not turn an older observation into a new one.
